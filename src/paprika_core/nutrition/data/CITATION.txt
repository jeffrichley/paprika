The nutrient data in this directory is a subset of USDA FoodData Central,
projected from the bulk CSV downloads by paprika_core.nutrition.bundle.

  Foundation Foods      2026-04-30
  SR Legacy             2018-04
  Survey (FNDDS)        2024-10-31

FoodData Central data are US Government works, in the public domain and not
copyrighted, released as CC0 1.0 Universal. No permission is needed for their
use. USDA requests, but does not require, the following citation:

  U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service.
  FoodData Central, 2019. fdc.nal.usda.gov.

Branded Foods is deliberately absent. It is 97% of the database, its records
outrank whole foods in every relevance ranking, and it would be 428 MB zipped.
